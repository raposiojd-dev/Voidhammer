The world in 30 seconds

Every kind of magic has a price.

A world broke into nine Realms. Each one has its own art, its own people and its own law, and every art takes something back from the one who uses it.

Lightforge

the Noonfolk · Meridian

Truth above everything. A lie that was believed is the one wrong past forgiving.

The priceRadiance costs Clarity. First you cannot keep a secret, then you cannot doubt, then you cannot forgive.

Shadowdeep

the Kept · Hush

The Realm of secrets. You give your full name to one person only.

The priceShadow Inversion erodes who you are: your reflection, then your handwriting, then your own name.

Stormreach

the Gale · Skyrend

Open sky and storm-riders who bow to nobody, and say so at volume.

The priceTempest ages the rider. Every full charge costs about a year.

Frostgrave

the Knotted · Rimehold

Time and kept promises, a knot on the cord for every one.

The priceChrona costs feeling. You feel a little less each time.

Stonehold

the Markers · Gravenhall

Endurance. The dead are carved on the mile-markers of the Long Roads.

The priceGravem turns you to stone, from the fingertips up.

Flameborne

the Kindled · Hearthmount

Defiance. Every child says their First No at thirteen.

The priceEmbercraft burns away self-control.

Spiritveil

the Veilborn · Lanthys Vale

Memory, and a lake of the dead’s lanterns. A law lapses when no one remembers why it was made.

The priceVeilweave bleeds memory: other people’s, until you can’t tell which are yours.

Metalcrown

the Forged · Iron Throne City

The shard with an empty hand. No Spark to wake, so they build one.

The priceSynthetic sparks cost your humanity, part by part.

The Unstable Shard

No people · no art

Raw possibility. The ninth piece, where reality could not hold.

The priceCollapsed at birth.

The Shattered Gate

The Voidhammer Saga · Book One

Emberhold is the last city standing. At its heart, a broken Gate that once opened onto eight other worlds has been dark for 109 years. Kaelren has buried forty-one squads and still bakes bread every week for the one he has now. Then the Gate looks back, and a man with no name and no memory falls out of the sky, covered in writing no one can read. Everyone wants to know what he is. The one person who might know isn't saying.
