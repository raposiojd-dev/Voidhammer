## Description
Nine Realms. One fractured world. And the Void that hungers for both.

Voidhammer is a sandbox war RPG in the spirit of the great medieval warband games, with a story, a cast of characters who matter, and a magic system where nothing is free.

It is the last season of the Concord. The Nine Realms still sit at one table under the Aetherguard High Council, Metalcrown is laughed out of the room, and Lightforge is drafting the Radiant Edict: every spark monitored, every scrap of Aether regulated, and one boy with two sparks kept under lock and key. You arrive just before it passes. You cannot stop the war that follows. You decide who lives through it, what gets remembered, and how the Realms go into it.

LEAD A WARBAND
Recruit from villages, train your troops through six tiers, feed and pay them, and march across a living map where realms wage war without you. Besiege towns and castles. Take them and rule them. Appoint vassal lords, call their banners and fight beside them in bigger battles.

MAGIC WITH A PRICE
Every realm has a school of magic, and every school takes something. Lightforge's Radiance costs troops their past. Shadowdeep erodes who you are. Stormreach ages its riders with every charge. Stonehold's stone creeps into the limbs. Flameborne burns away self-control. Your own power runs on memory, and a burned memory never comes back. Use it and you win the battle. Use it too much and you lose something that mattered.

NINE REALMS
Lightforge, Shadowdeep, Stormreach, Frostgrave, Stonehold, Flameborne, Spiritveil, Metalcrown, and the Unstable Shard, the ninth Realm that broke at its birth. Each has its own troops, architecture, politics and a crisis that unfolds whether you act or not. Befriend lords. Betray them. Watch them remember.

COMPANIONS WITH WOUNDS
Kaelren, the Flameborne King's young ward, never chooses himself. Elyndra, a Spiritveil Lantern archivist, writes down every promise you break. Jorren, a Shadowdeep exile, disappears and never explains. Noctis, the Iron Shade, only wants to be chosen. They make their own decisions, and they will surprise you.

THE VOID IS STIRRING
It is not evil, and it is not here yet. It has noticed something in the north. Rifts tear open, holds go grey, and the troops who touch it slowly vanish from your roster, names and faces first. Ignore it and you will be fighting on two fronts.

DEEP AND LONG
Dozens of hours of quests, sixteen boss lairs with their own stories, dungeons, a skill book with active battle skills, crafting, trade and a New Game Plus.

PLAYS OFFLINE. NO ACCOUNT. NO ADS. NO PURCHASES BEYOND THE GAME.
