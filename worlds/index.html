The Voidhammer Saga · The Worlds

There were nine Realms.
Eight have gone dark.

When the Hammer struck the First Spark, reality broke into nine pieces, and each one inherited a single fragment of power, and a price for using it. A Gate in Emberhold once opened onto all of them. It has shown nothing but stone for 109 years.

Realm I

Lightforge

Radiance Aether · truth, law, judgment

“All lies burn away beneath the First Light.”

The seat of the old Concord, where the Aetherguard High Council once sat. Lightforge believes corruption survives only where clarity is denied, and it built a whole Realm on that belief.

Belief
Truth is mercy before it is comfort.
Its flaw
Believing clarity and kindness are the same thing.

Realm II

Shadowdeep

Shadow Inversion · secrets, knives, silence

“Truth hides beneath light.”

Not one place but a network of tunnels and shadow roads running under every other Realm at once. Shadowdeep children are taught to keep their hands busy so no one sees them think.

Wielders
Nightblades and the spark-ripped Shroudborn
The price
Identity erosion. Use it long enough and you forget who was under the hood.

Realm III

Stormreach

Tempest Aether · storm, sky, speed

Freedom, movement, change.

Fortresses hung on chains of lightning over gulfs with no bottom. Stormreach and Stonehold have argued for a thousand years about whether anything should stay still.

Wielders
Sky riders who fight at the speed of weather
The price
Every charge ages the rider.

Realm IV

Frostgrave

Chrona Aether · time, ice, echoes, prophecy

Spoken promises must be kept. Forever.

The prophetic centre of the Realms. Its seers don't win battles; they decide which ones are survivable. Many ignored their warnings. History proved that was expensive.

Wielders
Frostwraith Seers, who see a few heartbeats ahead
The price
Feeling. Every future they look into leaves them a little colder.

Realm V

Stonehold

Gravem Aether · gravity, stone, endurance

Strength is not impact. It is what remains after impact.

Stonehold respects patience more than victory. Its oath-keepers anchor themselves like mountains, and a line they hold does not move.

Wielders
Gravem Oath-Keepers
The price
Their limbs slowly turn to stone.

Realm VI

Flameborne

Embercraft Aether · fire, rebellion, refusal

From ash, defiance rises.

A river of fire under a city whose windows are lit from below. Flameborne believes survival without defiance is just slower dying, and its magic rewards refusal. Its strongest wielders are often its angriest.

Wielders
The Emberforged
The price
It burns away self-control.

Realm VII

Spiritveil

Veilweave Aether · memory, ghostlight, Lantern Paths

What is remembered survives.

Lanterns on dark water, drifting in mist. Spiritveil's people are long-lived, and they keep the Lantern Archives. They don't preserve history; they preserve identity, which makes them the most dangerous Realm of all.

Wielders
Veilweave Archivists, who walk the Lantern Paths
The price
Memory bleed. Walk in enough minds and you start keeping pieces of them.

Realm VIII

Metalcrown

Synthetic sparks · machines, foundries, the Iron Dominion

“Power is not given. It is forged.”

The Realm that rejected Aether and built its own. The others laughed at Metalcrown and kept it off the Council. Metalcrown remembered.

Wielders
Anyone the foundries can fit with a spark
The price
Their humanity, one part at a time.

Realm IX

The Unstable Shard

Raw possibility

Reality cannot hold there.

The ninth Realm collapsed at its own birth. Maps leave it blank. Scholars argue about whether anyone has ever come back from it, and whether what came back was the same person.

Status
Never whole
The price
Unknown.

The last light

Emberhold

The Last Realm · the only city still standing

The other eight lights went out one after another, and the Gate at the heart of Emberhold went dark with them. For 109 years it has shown nothing but stone. A baker on the walls has buried forty-one squads and still makes bread every week for the one he has now.

This morning, the Gate looks back.
