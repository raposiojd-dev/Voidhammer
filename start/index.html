What is Voidhammer?

The last city

Emberhold was built in a hurry by people with nowhere else to go. At its heart stands a Gate that once opened onto eight other worlds. It has been dark for 109 years. The city walks around it like a grave.

The soldier who bakes

Kaelren doesn't age. He has buried forty-one squads, and every week he still gets up before dawn to bake bread for the one he has now.

The morning the Gate looks back

Something falls through the Gate. No name. No memory. Writing on his skin that no one can read. And Kaelren tells the first lie of his life.
