Voidhammer: The Shattered Realms — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

The short version

Voidhammer does not collect, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

What stays on your device

Your saves, settings and progress are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded. If you delete the app, your saves are deleted with it. The Backup Code feature creates text that you choose where to keep.

Co-op play

The optional co-op mode connects two devices directly using WebRTC. To find each other, the devices use a public signalling service (PeerJS), which briefly sees the temporary room code and the devices' network addresses, as any internet connection does. We do not receive or store this information. Co-op is only used if you choose to start or join a session.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children. It contains fantasy violence.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
