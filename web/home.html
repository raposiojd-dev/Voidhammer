Book One

The Shattered Gate

Emberhold is the last city standing. At its heart, forty feet of cracked arch stands on nothing. It used to open onto eight other worlds. Now people walk around it the way you walk around a grave.

Then the Gate screams, and something comes through: a man covered in writing no one can read. Every flame in the city leans toward him.
