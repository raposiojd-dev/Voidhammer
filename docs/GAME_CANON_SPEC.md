# GAME CANON SPEC — Voidhammer: The Shattered Realms
Approved by John (creator), 26 Oct 2026; public copy trimmed 7 Nov 2026. Sealed story material lives only in the private Project spec. The game must match this.

## ERA (John's ruling)
The game is set in the **late Ascension Era, just before the Radiant Edict**. The Nine Realms are still bound by the Concord and the Aetherguard High Council. Over the course of the game, the Edict is declared (Lightforge's law that all Aether is regulated, all sparks monitored, and the TwinSpark child controlled). Once it takes hold, **Metalcrown's Iron Dominion starts aggressively attacking everyone**, and the **Thousand-Year War begins**. The game's story ends as the war begins. The player cannot stop the war, because the books depend on it. The player's choices decide who survives, what gets remembered, and how the realms enter the war.

## THE ONE STORY SPINE (replaces the five separate finales)
1. **The Concord's last season.** Realm politics, crises and lords. Metalcrown is mocked and kept out of Council seats, and is quietly preparing.
2. **The TwinSpark child.** Rumours, then clues, then the truth: a boy carrying two sparks from all Realms, is the subject of the old prophecy. Every faction wants him.
3. **The Edict.** Ardyn Solbrand passes the Radiant Edict. Stormreach rebels, Flameborne riots, Stonehold refuses, Spiritveil warns, Shadowdeep sabotages, and Metalcrown smiles.
4. **The coup.** Metalcrown seizes the TwinSpark child. What happens next is sealed canon (see the private spec in the Project). The Dominion marches on Lightforge.
5. **Finale at the Iron Throne / the fall of Lightforge.** Existing ending choices (break / name / name-fails / take) become variations of one ending:
   - **"Name"** is the most canon-faithful. The player speaks the boy's true name (never shown on screen), and for one heartbeat the boy surfaces and spares them.
   - All variants close on **"The Thousand-Year War begins."** NG+ follows.

The Void finale, the Hollow Throne and the Emberhold "He Kneels" quest must stop being separate endings. Fold them into the spine as acts or side arcs (see below).

## THE VOID IN THIS ERA
The Void is stirring, not conquering. Before the coup it shows only as rare, faint **grey edges** (never "rifts" in player-facing text). Keep the Void systems, but frame them as early warning signs of the coming war.

## CHARACTERS (this era)
- **Kaelren**: a young **Flameborne** warrior, about 17, ward of the Flameborne King, who trained him ("Again, little ember"). Steady, notices what things cost, never chooses himself. **NOT Stormreach / "Stormborne".** **He cannot permanently die** (canon needs him alive): replace death with "carried from the field / wounded, returns later". The King's flame and oath are NOT given in the game, and the game does not hint at them.
- **The Flameborne King**: warm, generous, brings bread, scholarship patron, writes unsent letters to people he failed. Under strain as the Edict nears. Any "Ember King" content means him.
- **The TwinSpark child**: an Academy student who hides how much power he has. His name is sealed canon: never show it in game text, choices, comments or docs.
- **Noctis, the Iron Shade**: Metalcrown's first engineered machine-wraith, feared in children's stories. Can stay a freeable prisoner or companion; if held, the Dominion caught him only months before and never admits it.
- **The Eternal Voyager**: in this era he is alive and sits on the Aetherguard Council as the Hammer-Bearer, the one Councillor without a Realm. He is uncanny and kind. As an NPC he is fine; tone down "cannot die / void bargain not to feel" into "something the Codex cannot write". **His true name is unknown. Never give him one.**
- **Elyndra Vaelis**: a young **Spiritveil Lantern archivist** (long-lived), who keeps the Record, which is the **Lantern Codex**. **NOT a levy clerk in Ashford.**
- **Jorren**: Shadowdeep exile and spy who "disappears and never explains". **Keep him; this matches canon.**
- **Talla** (the game's Spiritveil lantern-scout hero): **rename the display name to "Wren"**. Canon's Talla lives 1,100 years later.
- **Aetherguard High Council**: Ardyn Solbrand (Radiance, passes the Edict), Seraphine Skyrend (Tempest, opposes it), Lyriel Frostwraith (Chrona, foresaw the collapse), the Silent Sentinel (Gravem), Kaelis Emberwarden (Embercraft), Vaelora Lanthys (Veilweave), the Voyager (Hammer-Bearer).
- **Metalcrown**: the Iron Dominus, the Synthesis Council, Chief Engineer Solvane (Iron Reaper).
- **The Archivist / Archivist Lume**: keep.
- **The Herald**: the Void's memory-eater. **The "Name-Eater" should be renamed to "the Herald".** **The battle-horn/banner item currently called "Herald" must be renamed** (e.g. "Warhorn") to avoid the clash.
- **Hunters**: constructs **made by the Codex** to hunt what it cannot write. Fix "made before the Codex".
- **"The Unwritten" boss**: rename the display name to **"The Unfiled"**. "The Unwritten" is reserved for the books.
- **Future villains** never appear by name in this era. Any old "Hollow King" content becomes a **prophetic vision** of an unnamed figure, clearly a future vision.

## WRITING RULES
Strict, concrete, warm, dry humour; keep the existing good jokes (the goat line).
- **Cut morals stated aloud.**
- **Avoid these:** "I do not know" spam, "for the first time" spam, "the weight of", "something shifted", and "Not X. Not Y." used more than once per scene.
- **Never spoil the main saga:** no Ember Oath detail beyond the final hint, no Kaelren immortality reveal, no Voyager true name, and no Consumption or Emberhold-as-last-refuge.

## LENGTH AND PACING (John, 29 Oct: "a 200-hour-plus game; the Void must not consume everything")
- **No calendar ending.** The story decides when it ends; the world keeps going after.
- **The Void is capped by story stage** (`ovVCap`): it can quiet 0 holds before day 150, then 1, 2 after the Edict, 3 after the coup, 4 after the finale. It never takes a capital, Iron Throne City or either of a realm's last two holds. Sealing the Rifts buys a 180-day lull, not a permanent end.
- **No realm is ever wiped out** by the Void, the Dominion or revolts; a realm always keeps its last hold. The Thousand-Year War has to start with every realm still standing.
- **The finale is earned**: Iron Throne City can't be assaulted until Acts V and VI are done, two realm sagas are complete and day 365 has passed (sandbox: the Dominion is marching and day 365 has passed).
- **After the finale** "Keep playing" continues the sandbox as the Thousand-Year War rages; New Game+ is optional, never forced.
